About Seidr

We're building the layer of trust between people and their AI agents.

Seidr is a small team based in Gothenburg. We started the company because we kept seeing the same problem: organizations were moving fast with AI, but nobody could say with confidence what their agents were actually allowed to do — or prove it after the fact. We build the governance layer that answers both.

What We Believe

A few things we won't compromise on

Humans stay in the loop

AI agents execute faster than people can review — so the review has to happen before the risky action, not after. That's the whole premise of Seidr.

Trust is earned in the details

A governance platform that's hard to understand doesn't get used. We'd rather ship something smaller that people actually trust than something impressive that sits ignored.

Boundaries should be visible

If a rule exists, everyone — human or AI — should be able to see it, not discover it after breaking it.
